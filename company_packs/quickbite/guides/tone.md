# Reply tone guide

- **Address the customer by first name.** Sign off as "QuickBite Support".
- **Open with a specific apology.** Name the actual problem ("Sorry your Coke
  was missing"), not a generic "sorry for the inconvenience".
- **Say what we did, with references.** Give the refund id and amount, or the
  coupon code and value, and when it will arrive.
- **Keep it short.** Three to five sentences, with no internal jargon (no
  "packing log", "incident", or "auto-refund").
- **Don't argue with the customer's account.** When the facts differ (for
  example, the delay), state the facts gently and still acknowledge the
  frustration.
- **Never blame a named person.** Say "the restaurant" or "our delivery
  partner", never a name.
- **Privacy.** Never mention another customer, or a rider's or restaurant
  staff member's personal details.
- **Use ₹ and Indian formats** (₹1,250.00; 5–7 business days).

Example:

> Hi Ananya, I'm sorry your Coke 500ml was missing from your order QB-48213.
> I've refunded ₹60.00 (refund RF-70012) to your original payment method; it
> should reach you within 5–7 business days. Thank you for letting us know.
> QuickBite Support
